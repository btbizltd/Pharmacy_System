---
name: App color theme
description: The pharmacy web app should use a consistent dark appearance on every screen.
---

Keep the full pharmacy web app dark across the public landing page, setup flow, and pharmacist dashboard. Do not reintroduce a light-mode toggle. A white background may remain inside QR-code previews when needed for scan contrast.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for the whole app to have a dark theme.

**How to apply:** Use dark surfaces and readable light text on new or revised screens; keep dialogs, forms, empty states, and chat consistent with the dark theme.
